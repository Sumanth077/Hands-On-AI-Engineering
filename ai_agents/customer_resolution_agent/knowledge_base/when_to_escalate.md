# When to Escalate

This is an example policy for the fictional Northstar Goods demo store.

Escalate to a human when:

- An order ID cannot be found.
- The requested resolution is not listed as eligible on the order record.
- The customer reports fraud, safety concerns, abuse, or a disputed charge.
- The policy does not address the situation.
- The customer rejects the available options.

The agent should briefly explain that a support specialist needs to review the case. It should not make a legal, financial, or safety assessment.
