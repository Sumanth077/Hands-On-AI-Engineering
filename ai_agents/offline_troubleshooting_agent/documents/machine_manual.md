# Machine A — Equipment Manual (Simulated)

This is a fictional equipment manual written for a simulated demo machine.
It does not describe any real industrial equipment.

## Normal Operating Ranges

Under normal operation, Machine A's sensors should read within the following
ranges:

- Temperature: 65–75°C
- Vibration: 1.5–3.0 mm/s
- Pressure: 4.5–5.5 bar
- Status: running
- Error code: none

Readings that drift outside these ranges, or a status of "warning" or "fault",
indicate a developing issue and warrant investigation before the condition
worsens.

## Error Codes

The following error codes may appear on Machine A's control panel. Each one
corresponds to a specific failure pattern and should be cross-referenced with
the troubleshooting guide for detailed inspection steps.

### BRG-02 — Bearing vibration warning

Symptoms:
- vibration significantly above the normal 1.5–3.0 mm/s range, often climbing
  toward 7–8 mm/s or higher
- temperature gradually rising above the normal range as friction increases
- pressure remains within its normal range

BRG-02 indicates the main shaft bearing assembly is showing signs of wear.
See the troubleshooting guide's bearing section for inspection steps.

### TMP-04 — Cooling system temperature warning

Symptoms:
- temperature rising well above the normal 65–75°C range, sometimes exceeding
  95°C
- vibration remains near its normal range
- pressure remains near its normal range

TMP-04 indicates the cooling system is not removing heat effectively. See the
troubleshooting guide's cooling section for inspection steps.

### PRS-03 — Pressure loss warning

Symptoms:
- pressure dropping below the normal 4.5–5.5 bar range, sometimes below 3 bar
- temperature may be mildly elevated above normal
- vibration remains near its normal range

PRS-03 indicates a loss of pressure somewhere in the system, most often from a
seal, hose, or fitting. See the troubleshooting guide's pressure section for
inspection steps.
