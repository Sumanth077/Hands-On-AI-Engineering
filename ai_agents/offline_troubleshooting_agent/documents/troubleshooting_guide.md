# Machine A — Troubleshooting Guide (Simulated)

This is a fictional troubleshooting guide written for a simulated demo
machine. It does not describe any real industrial equipment and should not be
used to service real machinery.

## Bearing Troubleshooting

Elevated vibration, especially when paired with a gradual temperature
increase and a normal pressure reading, points toward bearing wear (see
BRG-02 in the equipment manual).

Recommended inspection:
1. stop the machine according to normal shutdown procedure
2. inspect the bearing assembly for visible wear, play, or discoloration
3. inspect the lubrication condition and top up or replace lubricant if low
   or contaminated
4. replace the bearing if excessive wear is confirmed
5. after any repair, restart and confirm vibration and temperature return to
   their normal ranges before returning the machine to service

## Cooling Troubleshooting

A steadily rising temperature with vibration and pressure remaining near
normal points toward a cooling system problem rather than a mechanical fault
(see TMP-04 in the equipment manual).

Recommended inspection:
1. stop the machine according to normal shutdown procedure
2. inspect the cooling fan for obstruction or failure
3. inspect intake and exhaust vents for dust or debris blockage
4. inspect the coolant path (if equipped) for blockages or low coolant level
5. after clearing any obstruction, restart and confirm temperature returns to
   its normal range before returning the machine to service

## Pressure Troubleshooting

A drop in pressure, sometimes with mildly elevated temperature but normal
vibration, points toward a pressure system leak (see PRS-03 in the equipment
manual).

Recommended inspection:
1. stop the machine according to normal shutdown procedure
2. inspect seals for cracking, hardening, or visible leakage
3. inspect hoses and fittings for looseness or damage
4. inspect the pressure path for any obvious external leak
5. after resealing or replacing the affected part, restart and confirm
   pressure returns to its normal range before returning the machine to
   service

## Safety Disclaimer

This guide is for a simulated demo assistant only. It is not a certified
industrial safety system and does not replace trained maintenance staff or
the equipment's official documentation.

Always follow the machine's normal shutdown and maintenance procedure before
performing any inspection. Never bypass interlocks, disable alarms, or
continue operating equipment that is showing a fault condition. Recommended
actions in this guide are limited to inspections and standard maintenance
steps — never a reason to remove safety guards or override safety mechanisms
while a machine is running.
